# Privacy policy: Cordis Showcase

*Last updated: 7 October 2026*

Cordis Showcase is an iOS app that demonstrates the open-source [cordis-ios](https://github.com/eovidiu/cordis-ios) plugin framework. It is published by Ovidiu Eftimie.

## Data collection

**The app collects no data.** It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting, and it makes no network requests.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores one file on your device: the list of demo plugins and their settings, in the app's private Application Support folder. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Links

The About screen links to GitHub and arXiv. Tapping a link opens the page in Safari, and those sites' own privacy policies apply. The app does not send anything to them.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
